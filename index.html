Ticket Maker

Show tickets for the Comedy Castle box office, made in about two minutes a week instead of an afternoon.

Open Ticket Maker

For Comedy Castle office staff

This is a private tool for one comedy club's office. There is no sign-up and no public version. It is used by staff on the club's own computers, and it is here on the web only so it can be kept up to date without visiting the office.

What it replaces

Every show at the Comedy Castle needs printed tickets. Until now someone opened a design file, typed in the comedian, the day, the date, the time and the price, exported it, and repeated that for every performance — six or more in a busy week, and again whenever a show moved.

Ticket Maker automates this by scraping the Comedy Castle schedule and generating tickets automatically. A person still checks every show before anything is printed.

How it works

  1. Get the shows It reads the club's published schedule and lists what is coming up — this week, or the next four weeks.
  2. Check the list Every show can be corrected or removed. Comedy schedules change constantly, so nothing is ever treated as final.
  3. Create the tickets Each show becomes its own print-ready ticket, matching the ones the club already prints, in one folder ready for the box office.

The ticket it makes

Drawn from the club's real ticket stock — 5⅜ inches wide, orange bands top to bottom, the show details centred in the white.

MATT CONN THURSDAY, AUGUST 27, 2026 7:30PM +18 $26-$31
